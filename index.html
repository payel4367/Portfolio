A little about me

I’m a product designer. I make complicated products feel simple and easy to use.

Every project starts with questions: who is this for, where do they get stuck, and why. I look for answers in conversations, data and quick prototypes, then shape them into flows and interfaces that feel simple to use. I’ve done this across healthcare, fintech, travel, hiring, AI wellness and enterprise software.

  • 3+years designing products end to end
  • 6industries, from healthcare to fintech
  • 1dog who reviews every design

How I work, in five stops

  1. 01

    Listen first

    Interviews, surveys and usability sessions. I ask “why?” until even I’m a little tired of hearing it, because the real problem is rarely the one on the ticket.

    • User research
    • Usability testing
    • Stakeholder chats
  2. 02

    Untangle the mess

    Journey maps, personas and mental models turn a heap of notes into a clear picture of who’s stuck, where, and why.

    • Journey mapping
    • Personas
    • Information architecture
  3. 03

    Sketch it ugly

    Flows and wireframes, fast and rough. Ugly ideas are cheap to throw away, so I make lots of them before anything gets pretty.

    • User flows
    • Wireframes
    • Design thinking
  4. 04

    Prototype & poke

    Clickable prototypes go in front of real people. Where they hesitate, I iterate. A/B tests settle the friendly arguments.

    • Prototyping
    • A/B testing
    • Iteration
  5. 05

    Ship & sweat the details

    Pixel-tidy UI, a design system that keeps it consistent, and handoffs developers actually like. Then I watch the numbers and go again.

    • UI design
    • Design systems
    • Dev handoff
